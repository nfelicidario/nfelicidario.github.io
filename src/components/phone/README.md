# Phone kit: an Android phone with a Google Messages RCS conversation

Reusable, mock-data mockup pieces for case studies. Everything is JSX, lucide icons, and
phone-local CSS variables. No web fonts, no images, no network requests.

```tsx
import { AndroidPhone, MessagesHeader, ConversationPanel, DemoBanner, ThreadIntro, MessageBubble, Timestamp, RichCard, RichCardCarousel, SuggestionChips, Composer, TypingIndicator, AgentInfo, PANEL_PADDING, BANNER_CLEARANCE } from "@/components/phone";

<AndroidPhone brandColor="#1F4FE0" theme="auto" fit="contain">
  <MessagesHeader logo={<Logo />} name="Poblano's Mexican Grill" verified />
  <ConversationPanel composer={<Composer />} banner={<DemoBanner />}>
    <div className="flex min-h-0 flex-1 flex-col gap-2 overflow-y-auto" style={{ padding: `${BANNER_CLEARANCE}px ${PANEL_PADDING}px ${PANEL_PADDING}px` }}>
      <ThreadIntro logo={<Logo />} name="Poblano's Mexican Grill" description="Weekly deals and pickup orders." verified />
      <Timestamp>Today · 11:30 AM</Timestamp>
      <MessageBubble from="agent">Check out these sweet new deals at Poblano's Mexican Grill this week!</MessageBubble>
      <RichCardCarousel bleed={PANEL_PADDING} cardWidth="72%" cards={[{ title: "Family bundle", meta: "$29.99", description: "Four entrees, two sides.", media: <MyArt />, mediaHeight: "short", suggestions: [{ label: "👨‍👩‍👧 Feed the family", onSelect: ... }] }, ...]} />
      <SuggestionChips bleed={PANEL_PADDING} suggestions={[{ label: "Find location", kind: "location" }, { label: "View full menu", kind: "url" }, { label: "Reorder", emoji: "🔁" }]} onSelect={(i) => ...} />
      <MessageBubble from="user" status="read">I'll take the family bundle</MessageBubble>
      <TypingIndicator logo={<Logo />} />
    </div>
  </ConversationPanel>
</AndroidPhone>
```

Layout: the status bar, header, and gesture bar share the screen's darker ground (`--ph-bg`, the Material 3 surface-container tier). The conversation sits in `ConversationPanel`, a lighter panel that is full width, rounded at the top only, and runs to the bottom of the screen above the gesture bar. The thread (the intro, date, messages, cards, and chips in one scrolling column) fills the panel's upper region edge to edge and clips only at the panel's top and at the composer's divider; the demo banner floats over its top, and the composer is pinned at the bottom behind the divider, so the thread passes beneath both. Suggested replies sit inline under the message that offered them, not pinned to the bottom. `AgentInfo` is a full screen of its own, in the tonal look of the references: light ground, light system gray cards.

Logo mask: every logo slot (`MessagesHeader`, `ThreadIntro`, `AgentInfo`, `TypingIndicator`) is a square with `overflow: hidden`, a corner radius, and a transparent background, so it acts as a mask: a transparent PNG keeps its transparency, a round logo shows as a circle, and a square image gets its corners rounded. Render the image inside with `object-fit: contain`.

## Components

| Component | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `AndroidPhone` | The Pixel-style frame: thin bezel, 48px corners, centered pill cutout, status bar (time left, signal, wifi, and battery right), and the gesture pill. The screen is laid out at a fixed 360 x 780 logical px and zoomed to fit, so children always render at phone proportions. Props: `brandColor`, `theme` ("light", "dark", or "auto" to follow the site), `width` (CSS px; omit to fill the container), `fit` ("width" or "contain", which also respects the container height), `time`, `label`. Children render inside a flex column; make the thread `flex-1 min-h-0`. |
| `MessagesHeader` | Back arrow, the logo masked to a rounded square (36px, no background), the display name with the filled `VerifiedBadge` (16px) when `verified`, an optional `subtitle` status line (for "Preview", never "Verified business"; the badge says that), optional call icon, and overflow. Sits on the darker ground. |
| `ConversationPanel` | The lighter panel the thread lives in: full width, 24px top corners, flush with the bottom of the screen. Children fill the thread region edge to edge with no padding of their own from the panel: make the scroller a `flex-1 min-h-0 overflow-y-auto` column and pad it yourself with `PANEL_PADDING` (10) on the sides and bottom, and `BANNER_CLEARANCE` (40) on top when a `banner` floats over it. `banner` (usually `DemoBanner`) is absolutely positioned, centered, `BANNER_TOP` (8) from the top, so content scrolls beneath it; `BANNER_CLEARANCE` is `BANNER_TOP + BANNER_HEIGHT + 8` (58). Pass the composer as `composer`; it sits under a 1px divider (`--ph-outline-variant` at 55%) at the panel's bottom. Exports `PANEL_RADIUS`, `PANEL_INSET`, `PANEL_PADDING` (pass it to the chips and carousel as `bleed`), `BANNER_TOP`, and `BANNER_CLEARANCE`. |
| `DemoBanner` | A small neutral note that floats over the top of the conversation (pass it as `ConversationPanel`'s `banner`): "Demo agent. Create an account in RCS Studio to make it live." (`DEMO_BANNER_TEXT`; override with `text`). Tonal gray (`--ph-surface-high`) with a 1 px dark gray outline (`--ph-on-surface-variant` at 60%), 16 px corners, the info icon and the text as one group centered in the banner, the icon 8 px to the left of the text and vertically centered against the whole centered, balanced two-line text block. It spans the thread's content width (the panel padding is its side inset). `BANNER_HEIGHT` (42) is its height at the default copy. Show it only on a demo agent; a live or in-provisioning preview goes without it. |
| `VerifiedBadge` | Google's filled verified mark, the scalloped circle with a check, as an inline SVG. `size` (16 in the header, 20 in AgentInfo), `color` (defaults to the brand color, `--ph-brand`), `label` ("Verified"; pass "" to make it decorative). See the license note below. |
| `MessageBubble` | One message. `from="agent"` sits left in a neutral tonal container; `from="user"` sits right in the brand color (the only brand-colored thing in the thread). 28px corners; `tail` (default on) puts the 4px tail corner on the latest bubble. `status` ("sent", "delivered", "read") and `time` render under it. `ghost` draws a dashed placeholder. `Timestamp` is the centered day divider. |
| `RichCard` | Media on top (`mediaHeight` short 112, medium 168, tall 264; the references crop full-width media to 2:1, which is medium), title (17 px medium), optional `meta` (a price), `description` (16 px regular, on-surface; a string or your own nodes), and up to four suggestions as rows inside the card the way Messages draws them: a tonal card (`--ph-surface-high`, the agent bubble's tier) with `CARD_RADIUS` (20) corners and no outline, each suggestion a full-width lighter row (`--ph-surface`, `CARD_ROW_HEIGHT` 56, `CARD_ROW_RADIUS` 16) with the card's tone showing through as a hairline between rows, an action's icon (22 px) in a 40 px tonal circle at the left, then the label, left-aligned, in the plain on-surface color (neutral hover, not-allowed cursor when disabled). A reply label that starts with an emoji shows the emoji in the icon's place at the label's size. Nothing on the card reads the brand color. A suggestion's `overlay` renders inside its positioned button (a hint ring, say). `RichCardCarousel` lays out 2 to 10 cards in one row at Google's fixed widths (small 180, medium 296, the default, with the next card peeking in at the edge as Messages shows it) or at `cardWidth` (e.g. "72%" of the row), 8 px apart, every card stretched to the tallest; it scrolls natively on touch, by click-and-drag with the mouse (`useDragScroll`, shared with the chips), and snaps to a card when the gesture ends; `bleed` works as on the chips, so the next card peeks in at the panel's edge. `MediaPlaceholder` is the neutral gray gradient used when no media is given (`seed` varies the angle per card); pass `media` (an inline SVG drawn to the media area's aspect ratio, or an `<img>` with `object-fit: cover`) for real art. Suggestion labels are cut at 25 characters with `clampLabel`, which counts code points so an emoji in a label is never split. |
| `SuggestionChips` | The outlined pills Messages draws, in one horizontal row placed inline under the message that offered them; the row never wraps. A 1 px neutral outline (`--ph-outline-variant`), the label in the plain on-surface color at the body size (15 px regular), `CHIP_HEIGHT` (40) tall, 8 px apart; the row sits at the right while every chip fits (`align`, default "end", as Messages does) and starts at the left once it overflows. `kind` adds the action icon (`CHIP_ICON` 20, in the neutral system gray `--ph-on-surface-variant`, as the real threads draw it, never the brand or the accent): `dial` (phone), `url` (the lucide `Globe`, also in card actions), `location` (map pin), `share-location`, `calendar`; `emoji` puts an emoji in the icon's place on a reply chip (`emojiGlyph` renders it at the icon's size). `selected` fills the chip with the tonal gray; `disabled` dims the rest and shows the not-allowed cursor; enabled chips get a neutral hover (150 ms). Overflow scrolls with the scrollbar hidden: native pan on touch and pen, and click-and-drag with the mouse (`useDragScroll`). A plain click always taps the chip: pointer capture is taken only once the mouse has moved more than 4px (capturing on pointerdown would send the click to the row instead of the chip), and only then is the click after release swallowed. `bleed` (pass `PANEL_PADDING`) lets the row run to the panel's edge with matching padding, so chips clip at the edge, under a 32px fade on the right while there is more to scroll to and on the left once scrolled. Caps at 11 chips and 25 characters per label. |
| `Composer` | "+" attachment, the pill text field with the "RCS message" placeholder, camera, and gallery icons, and a neutral send button (dark circle, light arrow) once `text` is set. Lives inside the panel (`ConversationPanel`'s `composer` prop); the pill and the round button are the off-white `--ph-surface-high`. Decorative, nothing focusable. |
| `TypingIndicator` | The agent's small `logo` (24px, same mask) at the left, then three dots bouncing in a staggered wave (`TYPING_LOOP_MS`, 1.1 s, 140 ms between dots), on no bubble. The wave is a CSS `@keyframes` rule in a hoisted `<style>`, not a Motion loop, so it plays wherever the component mounts, including inside an `AnimatePresence initial={false}` (where Motion skips mount animations, a looping `animate` included). Static under reduced motion. Hold it at least 700 ms to show all three dots rise. |
| `ThreadIntro` | The top of a new business thread: the logo centered (`logoSize`, default `INTRO_LOGO_SIZE` 64, masked to a rounded square), the name with the 20px `VerifiedBadge` when `verified`, a two-line muted `description`, then a faint full-width divider. |
| `AgentInfo` | The agent details screen, as Messages draws it, scrolling on the light `--ph-surface-low` ground: a back arrow row; the 45:14 hero band (the 1440 x 448 banner, or a soft brand gradient) with the 72px logo overlapping its bottom edge, centered and masked; the name with the 22px `VerifiedBadge`; the description centered and muted (two lines); round tonal actions Call, Website, and Email; then rounded list cards in the light system gray of the surface-container tier (`--ph-surface-high`, not white): Verified ("The identity of this sender has been verified."), the phone number, the website, the email, each with its label as a subtitle, and View Privacy Policy and View Terms of Service with the external-link icon. Every value is optional; `placeholders` (defaults in `AGENT_INFO_PLACEHOLDERS`) stand in, muted, while a form is empty. |
| `useDragScroll` | The mouse drag-to-scroll hook behind the chips and the carousel: spread `handlers` on the scrolling element; `dragging` is true mid-drag. Touch and pen scroll natively. |

Tokens: `phoneTokens(brand, theme)` sets `--ph-brand`, `--ph-on-brand`, `--ph-accent` (the phone's own system accent, a fixed Google blue, kept for system chrome; chip and card icons are the neutral `--ph-on-surface-variant`), `--ph-bg` (the screen ground, surface-container tier), `--ph-surface` (the lighter conversation panel), `--ph-surface-low`, `--ph-surface-high`, `--ph-on-surface`, `--ph-on-surface-variant`, `--ph-outline`, `--ph-outline-variant`, and the frame colors. The surfaces are fixed neutral grays, not tinted toward the brand. Font stack inside the phone only: `Roboto, "Google Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`.

Brand color scope: `--ph-brand` is read in exactly three places, the hero band fallback gradient on `AgentInfo`, the `VerifiedBadge` fill, and the user's bubbles in `MessageBubble`. Everything else (agent bubbles, rich cards and their actions, chips and their gray icons, the composer, the typing dots, the media placeholder, and the RCS Studio card art) uses neutral tokens or a fixed palette, so changing the brand color moves only those three.

Keep site tokens (Tailwind `bg-surface` and friends) outside the phone, and phone tokens inside it. Nothing in the kit reads a site token.

## What an RBM conversation looks like (research notes, Oct 2026)

- Agent identity: display name up to 40 characters, description up to 100, a 224 x 224 logo under 50 KB shown as a rounded square since April 2026, a 1440 x 448 (45:14) banner under 200 KB, and a brand color (#RRGGBB) with at least 4.5:1 contrast on white. The brand color tints header accents and buttons. Verified agents get the check next to the name automatically.
- Agent info screen: banner with the logo overlapping it, name, description, phone (E.164), up to three labeled websites, an email on the brand's domain, and privacy policy and terms links (both required before launch).
- Bubbles: agent messages on the left in a neutral tonal container, the user's on the right in the primary (brand) color, pill-shaped with a small tail corner on the last of a run. Outgoing messages show sending, sent, delivered, and read states; read uses the filled double check. Agents can send a typing event so the thread shows the typing indicator.
- Rich cards: vertical cards put media on top at short 112, medium 168, or tall 264 dp (aspect 7:2, 21:9, or 3:2), then title (200 chars), description (2000), and up to four suggestions. Horizontal cards fix the media at 128 dp wide. Carousels hold 2 to 10 cards at small 180 or medium 296 dp wide, all scaled to the tallest.
- Suggestions: up to 11 per message, labels up to 25 characters, postback up to 2048. Replies are plain chips; actions carry an icon: dial a number, open a URL (browser or webview), view a location, share location, or create a calendar event. They render as outlined chips in a row above the composer.
- Composer: the placeholder reads "RCS message" on an RCS thread and "Text message" on SMS, with the attachment "+", camera and gallery icons, and a send button once there is text.
- Material 3: surface, surface container, and surface container high are the tonal tiers for the ground, panels, and floating cards; extended shapes use 28 dp corners; chips are 32 dp tall. Google Messages chips render as pills.
- Measured from real Google Messages RCS threads (Oct 2026, 360 dp wide): rich cards are tonal with no outline, about 20 dp corners, 8 dp side margins, full-width 2:1 media, a 17 dp medium title, a 16 to 17 dp regular description in the on-surface color, and suggestion rows 56 dp tall with 16 dp corners on a lighter surface, inset 16 dp, a 40 dp tonal circle around a 24 dp action icon; a carousel shows the 296 dp card with the next one peeking in, 8 dp apart; chips are about 40 dp pills with a 1 dp outline, a 20 to 24 dp icon in the neutral on-surface-variant gray, a 17 dp regular label, and sit at the right while they fit.
- System chrome on a Pixel: clock top left, signal, wifi, and battery top right, a centered camera cutout, and the thin gesture pill at the bottom. This is standard Pixel layout, not quoted from a spec page.

Not confirmed from a primary source within the research window: the exact "Verified business" string (third parties paraphrase it), exact bubble radii and color tokens, the composer icon order, and whether Messages uses Roboto or Google Sans for the thread. The kit uses sensible Material 3 values for those.
